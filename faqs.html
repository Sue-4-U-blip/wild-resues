In most cases, wild animals, even young ones, are best left undisturbed. However, you should reach out to a licensed wildlife rehabilitator if you come across:

An animal with visible injuries, such as a broken leg or wing. An animal that has been harmed by a domestic pet.

A young animal left alone for an extended time, suggesting it may be orphaned (even though the parents could be nearby).

An animal behaving strangely, such as a nocturnal species seen wandering during the day and showing signs of confusion.

♭ Research and planning♭

Allocate resources to studies that examine long-term survival of rehabilitated wildlife and their effects on the ecosystem.

Professionals must weigh the well-being of individual animals against the overall health of the ecosystem. For example, returning to a non-native species to the wild are typically discouraged.
